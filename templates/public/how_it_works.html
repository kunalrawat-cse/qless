How it works

From a phone tap to a collected meal

Five steps connect what students want with what the kitchen cooks.

  1. 1
    Student

    Pre-order

    Open the menu, search or pick a category, and add dishes to your cart. Each dish shows its Veg, Non-Veg or Egg label, allergens, prep time and how many are left.

  2. 2
    Student

    Choose a pickup slot

    Pick the next available 15-minute slot, or choose a later one today. Slots that are almost full are flagged, and full slots cannot be booked.

  3. 3
    Kitchen

    Kitchen preps

    Staff see orders grouped by pickup slot, plus the total of each dish needed in the next 15 minutes. One tap starts prep and one tap marks an order ready. Students see the status change live.

  4. 4
    Student and counter

    QR / OTP pickup

    Show your QR code or read out your 4-digit OTP at the counter. Staff verify it and hand over the meal. The code is saved on your phone, so it still works if the signal drops.

  5. 5
    Kitchen and manager

    Waste tracking

    At the end of a shift, staff log any wasted portions and the reason. Managers compare prepared against sold and watch the waste trend, so the next day's batch is better.
