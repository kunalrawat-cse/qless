About Qless

A campus canteen platform built to cut queues and food waste.

Why we built it

At lunchtime a campus canteen has two problems at once. Students wait in long queues, and the kitchen has to guess how much to cook. When the guess is too high, food is wasted. When it is too low, dishes sell out.

What Qless does

Students pre-order and pick a 15-minute pickup slot. The kitchen sees demand before it arrives, hands over meals with a QR code or OTP, and logs any waste at the end of the shift. Managers use that record to see what was prepared, what was sold and where waste happens.

Who it is for

Qless has three roles: students who order, kitchen and counter staff who prepare and hand over, and the canteen manager who manages the menu, pickup slots and reports.

About this project

Qless is a campus Design Thinking Lab project. It is a working version-one platform: the focus is a realistic, fast and usable single-canteen experience. Features such as demand forecasting are planned for later and are clearly marked as such.
