Sustainability

Match the kitchen to the demand

Food waste on campus often starts with guessing how much to cook. Qless replaces the guess with real order data.

What we measure, and what we aim for

Qless only reports numbers it can count from real orders and real waste logs. We do not publish estimates for CO₂ saved or trees planted.

Measured in the platform

Operational metrics

  • Waste todayPortions and kilograms logged by staff.
  • Prepared vs. sold efficiencyWhat share of the prepared batch was actually sold.
  • Surplus alertsUnsold items flagged near closing time.
  • Waste trendWaste per order over time, with the reason: overproduction, spoilage or uncollected.
Goals we work toward

What better data should lead to

  • Smaller, better-matched batchesKitchens prepare closer to what students pre-order.
  • A flatter lunch rushPickup slots spread demand, so food is not left waiting.
