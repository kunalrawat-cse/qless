Built for both sides of the counter

Better for students. Better for the canteen.

For students

  • Order ahead from your phone
  • Choose a 15-minute pickup slot
  • Follow your order: Confirmed, Preparing, Ready, Collected
  • Collect with a QR code or a 4-digit OTP
  • See Veg, Non-Veg, Egg and allergen labels before you order

For the canteen

  • See incoming orders grouped by pickup slot
  • Know what to prepare in the next 15 minutes
  • Mark a dish sold out in one tap
  • Verify pickups by QR code or OTP at the counter
  • Log waste and compare prepared against sold
